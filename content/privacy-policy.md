## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

- **Contact information:** your name, email address, phone number and company name when you make a booking, fill in a contact form or communicate with us
- **Booking details:** the services you book, the dates and times of your sessions, your chosen studio set and any add-ons you select
- **Payment information:** billing details processed through our payment providers. We do not store your full card details on our own systems
- **Communications:** messages you send us via email, WhatsApp, our website contact form or social media
- **Usage data:** information about how you navigate our website, including pages visited, time spent and referring sources, collected via cookies and analytics tools
- **Media content:** video and audio recordings made during your studio sessions, which are your content and remain yours

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

- Process and confirm your studio bookings
- Communicate with you about your sessions, including reminders and follow-ups
- Process payments and issue invoices
- Deliver your recorded files and edited content
- Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
- Improve our services and website experience
- Send you information about our services where you have consented to receive it
- Comply with our legal obligations under UAE law

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases in accordance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL):

- **Contract performance:** to fulfil your booking and deliver the services you have paid for
- **Legitimate interests:** to operate and improve our business, communicate with clients and maintain our records
- **Legal obligation:** where we are required to process data to comply with UAE law
- **Consent:** where you have given us clear permission, such as to receive marketing communications

## 5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with:

- **Payment processors:** to handle transactions securely
- **Cloud storage providers:** such as Google Drive, for secure file delivery of your content
- **Booking platform providers:** the tools we use to manage appointments and calendars
- **Professional advisors:** accountants, lawyers and auditors where required
- **Government authorities:** where required by UAE law or legal process

All third-party providers we work with are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy:

- **Booking and payment records** are kept for a minimum of five years in accordance with UAE commercial law
- **Recorded media files** delivered via Google Drive are available for download for 7 days after delivery, after which they may be deleted from our systems
- **Raw footage** is stored for 30 days after your session date
- **Marketing communications data** is retained until you withdraw your consent

## 7. Your Rights

Under UAE data protection law you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data
- Request deletion of your data where it is no longer necessary
- Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
- Lodge a complaint with the UAE Data Office

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [hi@birdcast.me](mailto:hi@birdcast.me). We will respond within 30 days.
